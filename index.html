Transportation

Make routes clear before visitors arrive.

From Manila

Overnight bus to Sorsogon City or travel via Bicol airport, then van or bus onward.

Sorsogon City to Donsol

Use van, jeepney, private car, or arranged tour transfer depending on schedule.

Sorsogon City to Bulusan

Take buses or vans toward the east coast, then local rides to the lake area.

Matnog islands

Travel to Matnog port, then book local boats for island hopping and beach stops.
